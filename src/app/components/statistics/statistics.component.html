Understanding the Statistics

Hand Size Logic

A hand that can cast Etali with 4 cards counts as a success for hand sizes 4, 5, 6, and 7. This represents "can I cast Etali if I mulligan down to N cards?"

Confidence Intervals

The 90% confidence interval shows the range where the true probability likely falls. Wider intervals indicate less certainty (fewer tested hands), while narrower intervals indicate more certainty (more tested hands).

Overall Mulligan Probability

This is the most important statistic - it answers: "What's my chance of casting Etali by turn 2 in an actual game after using my mulligans?"
